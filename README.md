# Teaching Materials for Discrete Methods in Modern AI

This repository contains the teaching materials for the "Discrete Methods in Modern AI" course given at the University of Tübingen (Winter Semester 2026/2027).

### Description 

In this course, we examine applications of discrete modeling techniques to problems in modern language modeling and generative AI. We will study how such techniques—drawn from logic, automated reasoning, and classical probabilistic inference—can support deeper semantic analysis of current systems, provide a means of incorporating structured knowledge and constraints into learning and inference, and offer theoretical tools for characterizing their capabilities and limitations. More specifically, we will introduce methods for SAT solving, tractable logical and probabilistic inference, and probabilistic programming, focusing on their applications to next-token modeling, learning with semantic constraints, preference-based post-training, prompting, and test-time inference.
